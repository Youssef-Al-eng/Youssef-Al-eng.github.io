About

I am an Applied AI Engineer based in Cairo, Egypt. I build AI systems end to end — from data preparation and model development to APIs, interfaces, and deployment — and I care about systems that work in real environments, not only in notebooks.

My background combines Electrical and Electronics Engineering with hands-on machine learning engineering. That mix shows up in my projects: computer-vision inspection systems for manufacturing lines, retrieval-augmented (RAG) assistants over real documents, predictive-maintenance platforms trained on thousands of inspection records, and embedded IoT devices that sense and stream data from the physical world.

What I work on

  • Applied machine learning and deep learning for real products
  • Computer vision for industrial inspection (YOLOv8, depth sensing, real-time pipelines)
  • Generative AI, LLMs, and RAG (LangChain, LangGraph, FAISS, ChromaDB, Qdrant)
  • AI-powered applications and multi-agent systems
  • Healthcare and industrial AI
  • Embedded AI, IoT, and intelligent monitoring (ESP32, sensor fusion, cloud dashboards)

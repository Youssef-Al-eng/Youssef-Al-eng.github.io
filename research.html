GenAI-NetOpt — Cross-Layer Protocol Optimization via Generative AI

Generative AI · Networking · Manuscript submitted to IEEE Potentials (2026)

A unified cross-layer framework embedding generative AI modules into the protocol stack: LLM-based traffic prediction (Mistral 7B), CVAE-PPO congestion control, a fine-tuned LLM router, multi-agent MAC scheduling, and DQN-based edge offloading — with a Streamlit demo.

Python · PyTorch · LLMs · Reinforcement Learning · Streamlit

AdaptiveQoS-LLM — Cross-Layer QoS Classification

Deep Learning · Networking · IEEE Potentials submission (2026)

A cross-layer transformer that classifies network traffic into fine-grained QoS classes by learning joint representations across protocol layers — from physical-layer SNR/BER through MAC statistics and routing metrics to TCP congestion-window dynamics.

Python · PyTorch · Transformers

PINN for Structural Steel Alloys

Physics-Informed Neural Networks · Materials · Research code for a paper submitted to Next Materials (Elsevier)

Physics-informed neural networks predicting yield strength, UTS, and elongation of structural steel alloys, embedding Hall-Petch and thermodynamic constraints directly into the training objective.
